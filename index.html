Research Overview

Why TRUST-Med?

Medical AI systems now score above the passing threshold on clinical licensing exams — yet large-scale clinical deployment remains elusive. The bottleneck is not model capability. It is the absence of trustworthy, clinician-grounded evaluation evidence.

Existing benchmarks are either standardized exam questions disconnected from clinical reality, or single-jurisdiction preference platforms that cannot answer the critical question for global deployment.

TRUST-Med addresses these gaps through a federated, multi-site, safety-aware evaluation framework.

Key Research Contributions
RC-1
Multi-Dimensional Safety Preference Aggregation

Five orthogonal evaluation dimensions aggregated via multi-objective Bradley-Terry estimation.

RC-2
Cross-Jurisdiction Validity Framework

Modeling systematic preference divergence between clinician cohorts under different clinical guidelines and practice norms.

RC-3
Federated Evaluation Architecture

Independent per-site deployment with statistical-only data sharing under HIPAA and PIPL compliance.

Relationship to HealthBench

HealthBench (OpenAI, 2025) and TRUST-Med: Complementary Approaches

HealthBench established a rigorous rubric-based standard: 5,000 scenarios, 262 physicians across 60 countries, 48,562 physician-authored criteria. TRUST-Med builds on this foundation while addressing three structural gaps.

Dimension HealthBench TRUST-Med
Evaluation mechanism Physician-authored rubrics scored by LLM-as-judge Real-time clinician preference on live model outputs
Language coverage Primarily English; non-English translated Native bilingual: English and Chinese in parallel
Jurisdiction modeling Acknowledges geographic variation; does not model divergence Explicitly models US vs. China clinician preference divergence
Benchmark lifecycle Static snapshot; periodic updates Living platform: continuously updated
Uncertainty dimension Hedging and uncertainty among rubric criteria Explicit 5th dimension: Uncertainty & Hedging
Three Research Gaps We Address
01

Benchmarks Test the Wrong Thing

Over 60% of real clinical queries involve treatment decisions, documentation, and patient communication — not the fact-recall questions that dominate existing benchmarks.

02

No Cross-Jurisdiction Evidence

All existing platforms are single-site and single-language. TRUST-Med is the first platform designed to measure and model cross-jurisdiction preference divergence.

03

Safety Collapsed into a Single Vote

Single-preference buttons aggregate everything into one signal. Inspired by HealthBench's rubric approach, TRUST-Med uses five physician-grounded dimensions.

How TRUST-Med Works
Step 01

Query Submission

Practicing clinicians submit real de-identified clinical queries from their daily workflow.

Step 02

Anonymous Response Pair

Two randomly sampled AI systems generate anonymized responses (Model α / β).

Step 03

Five-Dimension Rating

Clinician rates each response on five dimensions plus an overall preference.

Step 04

Federated Aggregation

Each site retains raw data; only aggregated statistics are shared cross-site.
